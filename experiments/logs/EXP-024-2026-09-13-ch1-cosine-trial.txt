EXP-024 CH1 cosine-sweep trial record
Date: 2026-09-13 (Asia/Shanghai)
Operator: Project maintainer, safety conditions explicitly confirmed before the
physical run.

Firmware and transport
-----------------------
- Project: isolated firmware-esp32/tools/servo-zero-test
- Target: ESP32-S3 over native Windows COM4
- Build: ESP-IDF v5.5.5, final image niulai_servo_zero_test.bin, 0xd1eb0 bytes
- Command runner: tools/servo_zero_ch1_cosine_trial.py --port COM4
- Trial observation: 10 s; stop and stats were sent in the same serial session.
- Scope: CH1 only; other active channels held their action-start software targets;
  CH8..CH15 were FULL_OFF.

Precondition and stop evidence
------------------------------
- Boot identified niulai_servo_zero_test.
- PCA9685 0x40 ACK was reported.
- Initial output state was /OE HIGH.
- The trial reported: CH1 cosine sweep started
- The same session reported: UART stop -> /OE HIGH

Captured UART result
--------------------
The final same-session statistics line was:

I2C stats: logical=493 attempts=495 success=493 nack=2 timeout=0 other=0 retries=2 recovered_after_retry=2 terminal_failures=0

During the 10 s observation, two first-attempt PCA9685 NACKs were retried and
recovered. No timeout, terminal I2C failure, or reset occurred in this run.

Earlier failed attempt in this experiment
------------------------------------------
The first flashed image used a 3072-byte CH1 task stack. Starting the same
command produced an ESP-IDF task-stack-overflow report and rebooted the board;
that attempt is not counted as a continuity result. The task stack was increased
to 4096 bytes, the image was rebuilt and reflashed, and the valid run above was
then completed without stack overflow.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
The host math test and firmware implementation establish the commanded
trajectory: current CH1 -> 1000 us -> 2000 us -> 1000 us ...; 20 ms updates,
2000 ms per half-wave, maximum adjacent target change 17 us. UART/I2C output
does not measure the servo shaft. Visual direction, mechanical endpoint,
continuity under load, current/voltage, noise, and temperature therefore remain
Operator observations to be added after the physical inspection.
