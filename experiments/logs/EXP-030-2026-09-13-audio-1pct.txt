EXP-030 1% embedded MP3 device trial
Date: 2026-09-13
Hardware: ESP32-S3 QFN56 rev v0.2; native Windows COM4; USB-Enhanced-SERIAL CH343
Firmware build: /tmp/niulai-audio-1pct
Configuration: CONFIG_NIULAI_AUDIO_VOLUME_PERCENT=1
I2S: BCLK=GPIO9, WS/LRC=GPIO10, DIN=GPIO4; MP3 first frame 44100 Hz stereo
Command: audio play

Precondition:
- Bootloader, application, and partition table were flashed with esptool 4.12.0.
- All three regions reported Hash of data verified; hard reset completed.
- The first attempt produced no audio command acknowledgement because the normal
  UART loop exited on an empty non-blocking VFS read. app_main.c was then changed
  to non-blocking fgetc polling and the image was rebuilt and flashed again.
