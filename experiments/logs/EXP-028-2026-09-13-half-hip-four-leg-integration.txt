- Quad demo channel order: RF CH0/1, LF CH2/3, RR CH4/5, LR CH6/7
- Final state after every trial: UART stop -> /OE HIGH

Trial A: keyboard sequence 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4
---------------------------------------------
- Host command: quad_ascii_walk.py --port COM4 --profile
  30,9,40,120,360,120,50 --sequence 1234
- Startup: direct rest hold reached; direct rest ready; /OE LOW
- RF mask 0x01: accepted; complete; lift=40.0 mm; hip_scale=50%;
  soft_limit_legs=0x00
- LF mask 0x02: accepted; complete; lift=40.0 mm; hip_scale=50%;
  soft_limit_legs=0x00
- RR mask 0x04: accepted; complete; lift=40.0 mm; hip_scale=50%;
  soft_limit_legs=0x00
- LR mask 0x08: accepted; complete; lift=40.0 mm; hip_scale=50%;
  soft_limit_legs=0x00
- The first RF accepted response arrived after the host's first 120 ms
  window; the repaired host retried once. The duplicate press was idempotent
  and did not cancel the trajectory.
- No-reset post-run stats: logical=198 attempts=198 success=198 nack=0
  timeout=0 other=0 retries=0 recovered_after_retry=0 terminal_failures=0
- Final: UART stop -> /OE HIGH
- UART proves the four logical trajectories and communication path completed;
  it does not prove body translation, foot slip, mechanical clearance, current,
  temperature or absence of a physical fall. No video/visual observation was
  captured in this log.

Trial B: diagonal pair sequence 0x06 -> 0x09
---------------------------------------------
- Host sent, after direct rest: demo quad press 6 PROFILE, release 6, then
  demo quad press 9 PROFILE, release 9, and stop.
- mask 0x06 = LF+RR started and was accepted with hip_scale=50%.
- At about 270 ms, the active atomic frame encountered repeated PCA9685
  hardware NACK. Four retry attempts were exhausted and the firmware stopped
  the quad demo and raised /OE HIGH.
- Stats after the aborted pair: logical=44 attempts=48 success=43 nack=5
  timeout=0 other=0 retries=4 recovered_after_retry=1 terminal_failures=1
- mask 0x09 was rejected because the failed first pair cleared rest-ready
  state; it is not a result for the RF+LR pair.
- Final: UART stop -> /OE HIGH
- No UART fall signal exists in this isolated demo. The terminal NACK is a
  communication/power or load-boundary observation, not evidence that a
  mechanical fall occurred. Do not repeat this two-leg high-load trial until
  the PCA9685/servo supply, common ground and mechanical load are checked.

Conclusion
----------
The half-hip profile is integrated into the four-leg path and passes the
single-leg-at-a-time total-assembly UART sequence. It does not yet pass the
two-diagonal-leg high-load experiment because of one terminal NACK sequence;
the gait is therefore still an experimental candidate, not accepted hardware
walking behavior.
