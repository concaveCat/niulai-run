Root cause:
  formal carrier order is FR=CH0/1, FL=CH2/3, RR=CH4/5, RL=CH6/7;
  compatibility pose_map still treated ACTIVE_LEG_FL as CH0. The configured
  1200 us lift target therefore violated CH0's reviewed 1400 us minimum.

Fix:
  pose_map now uses semantic index 0=FL,1=FR,2=RL,3=RR and carrier bases
  {2,0,6,4}; the compatibility command mask uses semantic leg index rather
  than channel-pair index. No hard pulse envelope or gate threshold was widened.
  Added host regression asserting FL -> CH2/CH3 and lift 1200/1700 us.
